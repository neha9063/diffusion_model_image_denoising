Discussion of Results & Model Limitations (Viva Preparation)
Noise vs. Quality Trade-off

At small timesteps (t < 100), the signal retention factor √(ᾱt) is high, so reconstruction achieves >25 dB PSNR. At higher timesteps (t > 250), the original pixels are almost entirely replaced by Gaussian noise, testing the true generative stroke hallucination of the U-Net.

Inference Latency

Because reverse diffusion is an iterative Markov chain requiring t sequential neural network forward passes, reverse sampling takes noticeably longer than single-step autoencoders. This illustrates the fundamental trade-off in generative diffusion models between sample quality and sampling speed.

Dataset Domain Specificity

The model is trained strictly on 28×28 grayscale digits. If natural RGB photographs are supplied, the system projects them to this low-resolution grayscale space. Denoising natural photos requires multi-channel models trained on datasets like CIFAR-10, ImageNet, or CelebA.
